Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At LO$TKART, accessible from www.lostkart.in, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by LO$TKART and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register on our platform, report an item, or submit a claim, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Account Data: Your name, email address, phone number, and college roll number.
  • Item Data: Descriptions, locations, and photographs of lost or found items you upload.
  • Verification Data: Distinctive features, hidden marks, or images of receipts provided when claiming an item to prove ownership.
  • Log Data: IP addresses, browser type, and timestamps for security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our lost and found portal.
  • Verify ownership of items before they are handed over.
  • Communicate with you regarding your reports or claims.
  • Prevent fraudulent claims and maintain the security of our platform.
  • Send you emails regarding updates or customer support queries.

3. Data Security & Verification

Your privacy is highly important to us. The "Proof of Ownership" details you submit when claiming an item are kept strictly confidential. They are only visible to the LO$TKART administrators for verification purposes and are never displayed publicly on the website.

We use industry-standard encryption for passwords and secure cloud storage (via Cloudinary and MongoDB Atlas) to protect your uploaded images and account data.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may use third-party service providers (like Cloudinary for image hosting and MongoDB Atlas for database management) to help us operate our business and the Site.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, or to request that we correct or delete your data. You can update your profile directly from your dashboard or contact us at support@lostkart.in for account deletion.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If we update our policy, we will post those changes on this page.

Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@lostkart.in.
