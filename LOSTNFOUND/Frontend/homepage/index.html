ABOUT LO$TKART

LO$TKART is a Lost and Found website that helps people find their lost items easily.

What is LO$TKART?

LO$TKART is a simple Lost and Found platform. It helps users report lost items and search for items that have been found.

Our Aim

Our aim is to connect people with their lost belongings and make the Lost and Found process simple and easy.

What We Provide

  • Report Lost Items
  • Search for Lost Items
  • Find Found Items
  • Easy to use website

HOW IT WORKS

Follow these simple steps to find or report your lost items.

1

Report an Item

Report your lost or found item by adding its name, category, location and other details.

2

Search & Match

Search through the available items and check the details to find a possible match.

3

Get Your Item Back

Contact the person who reported the item and safely collect your lost belongings.
